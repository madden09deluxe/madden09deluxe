Put this folder inside your JSGME Mods folder 
("MODS" is the default folder, JSGME and "MODS" folder must be next to your textures folder for PCSX2.)

The structure of the mod should be like this:

[name of mods folder]\[name of mod]\textures\SLUS-21946\replacements\a-[nameofmod]

EXAMPLE:

C:\pcsx2-v1.7.4029-windows-64bit-AVX2-Qt\MODS\Super Bowl LIX - PHI vs KC\textures\SLUS-21946\replacements\a-superbowl lix
